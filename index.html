See how we build
  1. 01

    Frame

    A rack-scale system starts as a 48U chassis, rails and rear busbar. Every position is planned before the first tray arrives.

  2. 02

    Power and coolant distribution

    A DC busbar and two liquid manifolds run the full height of the rack. Supply on one side, return on the other.

  3. 03

    Compute trays

    Eighteen compute trays slide in on rails. Each carries Vera CPUs and Rubin GPUs on a shared cold plate.

  4. 04

    NVLink switch fabric

    Nine switch trays in the middle of the rack join every GPU into one memory domain. No optics, no top-of-rack hop.

  5. 05

    Liquid cooling loops

    Each tray connects to the manifolds with quick-disconnect couplings. Direct-to-chip liquid removes the heat that air cannot.

  6. 06

    Coolant distribution unit

    The CDU pumps coolant through the loops and rejects heat to the facility water. Flow starts before the first workload does.

  7. 07

    Power on. Then scale out.

    One rack becomes a row. Our orchestration layer runs on top of the metal from the first day of deployment.
